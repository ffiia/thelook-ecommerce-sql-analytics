# TheLook E-Commerce: Data Analytics Portfolio
## Project Overview

This project uses transaction and user behaviour data from the public theLook eCommerce dataset in Google BigQuery to explore business questions. It presents key findings and practical recommendations for the Product, Marketing, CRM teams, and business manager.

---
## Business Questions
This project explores five business questions across various departments:
* **Product Portfolio Profiling (Product Dept.):** What are the key characteristics of our current inventory in terms of categories, departments, brands, and retail prices?
* **Premium Product Identification (Marketing Dept.):** Which high-tier products are priced above $100 and suitable for exclusive marketing campaigns?
* **Inventory Volume Distribution (Product Dept.):** Which product categories dominate our warehouse storage with the largest number of products?
* **Customer & Order Mapping (CRM Dept.):** Who are our active shoppers and what are their specific order timelines and transaction statuses?
* **Financial Performance Tracking (Business Unit Manager):** Which product categories contribute the largest revenue share based on actual item-level sales?
